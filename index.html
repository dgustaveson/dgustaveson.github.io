How to use this survey

  1. Read each statement as it appears on screen.
  2. Choose one:
    • Agree — you support the statement
    • Disagree — you don't support it
    • Pass — skip it if it's unclear, confusing, or you're not sure
  3. Want to add your own short statement? Type it in the box and submit. Other participants can then vote on it the same way.
  4. After at least 7 people have voted, you'll see a display of clustered opinion results. The blue dot is you. Once the chart appears, you'll see shapes representing groups of people who tend to vote the same way — these are called opinion clusters. Your own position is marked with a blue dot, so you can see which group you're closest to, or whether you land somewhere in between.

Notes:

  • Votes are anonymous.
  • You can't reply directly to someone else's statement — You can only vote on these statements.
  • There's no wrong answer. Passing is fine.
