How to use this survey

  1. Read each statement as it appears on screen.
  2. Choose one:
    • Agree — you support the statement
    • Disagree — you don't support it
    • Pass — skip it if it's unclear, confusing, or you're not sure
  3. Keep voting on as many statements as you'd like. There's no limit.
  4. You'll see a chart of everyone's results once at least 7 people have voted. Until then, the chart won't be visible — this isn't about how many statements you personally vote on, it's about how many total people have joined in.
  5. Want to add your own short statement? Type it in the box and submit. Other participants will then vote on it the same way.

Notes:

  • Votes are anonymous.
  • You can't reply directly to someone else's statement — You can only vote on these statements.
  • There's no wrong answer. Passing is fine.
